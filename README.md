# Beauty Bloom Hub

1

Set Up Branding & UI

Create the Beauty Bloom identity with sidebar navigation and bilingual support.

Design logo with 'Beauty Bloom' and pink rose integrated into the bloom

Sidebar navigation on the left with Home, Services, Food Ordering, Collaborations, Advertising, Partner Shops, Policies, Gallery

Add language toggle for English and IsiXhosa

Place search bar at the top and cart button beside it

02

Implement Booking & Services

Enable customers to book appointments and add services to cart.

Build service catalogue with add-to-cart functionality

Add cancellation feature: only 24h before appointment, with fees

Track bookings in database; after 10 successful bookings, apply 10% discount

Offer free beverages (tea, coffee, water, hot chocolate, biscuits) as cart add-ons

03

Food Ordering Integration

Allow customers to order food during appointments.

Add food partners: KFC, McDonald’s, Burger King, The Poke Co., Hungry Lion

Customers select food and time; fee added to appointment payment

Salon places order at specified time

04

Brand Collaborations

Provide interface for brands to work with Beauty Bloom.

Collaboration feature with contact details

Rate card: Facebook posts (R500 pictures, R800 videos), Instagram (R600 pictures, R900 videos), TikTok (R700 videos)

Brands specify requirements and campaigns

05

Advertising Bookings

Enable external businesses to promote products at the salon.

Booking feature for in-person promotions

Fee: R500/day for advertising and sample distribution

Collect business contact details

06

Payments & Loyalty

Integrate payment options and loyalty rewards.

Support all South African banks, PayFlex, PayJustNow

Cart system applies discounts after 10 bookings

Track customer database via login/registration

07

Partner Shop Recommendations

Suggest other shops and manage commission.

Recommend shops offering different services

Clients book and pay directly

Beauty Bloom deducts 15% commission

08

Media & Slideshow

Showcase services and contact details visually.

Slideshow with real photos (not AI-generated)

Slides: services catalogue, contact details, trading hours, social media buttons

Trading hours: Mon–Fri 09:00–18:00, Sat 09:00–17:00, closed Sun & public holidays

09

Policies & Extras

Add policies and interactive features.

Cancellation & late arrivals policy page

Rise anime feature for playful UI animations

Competitions feature: fake competition with visible participant list

Add contacts in sidebar for quick access

This roadmap ensures Beauty Bloom’s app is feature-rich, customer-friendly, and business-ready. It combines AI productivity principles (automation, personalization, responsible design) with salon-specific needs like food ordering, brand collaborations, and loyalty rewards.
